# Changelog — August 24–30, 2026

Smarter quantities and nicer YouTube recipes.

## ✨ New features

- **YouTube recipes get a thumbnail** — pasting a YouTube link now shows its thumbnail as the recipe photo automatically.

## 🐛 Fixes

- **Shopping list quantities you can actually read** — combined amounts now show as nice fractions (e.g. 1/2 instead of 0.5), rounded sensibly, with no pointless unit conversions.
- **Editing a recipe no longer loses data** — notes and details are preserved on save.
- Clearer quantity units (regionalized vs. standard).
- Sidebar: new collapse button, fixed theme toggle and meal-card styling.

## 💅 Improvements

- Simpler feedback buttons.
- General small UX polish.
