# Changelog — July 18–26, 2026

A big foundation week for finding and cooking recipes.

## ✨ New features

- **Server-side recipe import with credits** — importing a recipe from a URL now runs on the server and uses credits, which makes imports faster and more reliable.
- **Recipe filters** — filter recipes with multiple criteria in a mobile-friendly sheet/drawer.
- **Know what you can cook** — recipe cards now show a cookable status based on what you already have in your groceries.
- **Supporter status in the app** — supporters now see a banner and their status directly in the app (hidden again after 2 months, no nagging).
- **Recipe carousel wraps** — browse recipes without hitting a dead end.

## 💅 Improvements

- Cleaner recipe carousel navigation.
