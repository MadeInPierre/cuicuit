## Welcome to Cuicuit!

Hi! I'm Pierre 😊 I built Cuicuit for myself and thought it might be useful to more people: an app that takes the mental load out of groceries. Throw in meal ideas whenever you think of them, add missing items, and Cuicuit merges everything into one shopping list sorted the way you walk the store — and tells you which meals you can already cook. 

Open source, free to use, and this is only the beginning. Thank you so much for checking it out! I'd love to chat about what you think on [Discord](https://discord.gg/guQDECXK7) 🦚.

## Highlights - Available Now

- **Import any recipe in seconds** — paste a link, a video, a photo, or plain text.
- **Meal plan with no calendar** — add meals the moment you think of them.
- **Real-time combined shopping list** — merged, grouped by aisle, readable quantities.
- **Know what you can cook** — every meal shows whether you already have what it needs.
- **Free, and great on your phone** — install it as an app and take it to the store.

### Import any recipe in seconds

Found a recipe on a blog, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, or a handwritten note? Paste the link, snap a photo, or paste the text — Cuicuit pulls out the ingredients, quantities, steps, and timings, and guesses the filters for you. Pasting a YouTube link even grabs the video thumbnail as the recipe photo automatically. 

Imports show a live progress stepper so you can watch it happen, and re-importing a link you've used before is instant. Only imports use community "seeds" (1 per import, charged after success — failures are free), and everything else in the app is unlimited.

![Import banner](/changelog/v0.1.0/import_banner.png)

### A meal plan with no calendar

Most planners make you fill a week grid. Cuicuit doesn't: you drop a recipe or a missing item into your plan whenever the idea strikes — Sunday night or in the checkout line. No dates, no ceremony. When something's cooked, mark it and move on.

![Plan and extras](/changelog/v0.1.0/plan.png)


### One shopping list that sorts itself

Every planned meal and extra item merges into a single list, grouped by supermarket aisle so you walk the store once. Quantities combine into readable fractions (1/2, not 0.5), and if your store lays out aisles differently, set your own aisle order and the list follows it. Each item stays linked to its meal, so you always know what it's for.

![Merged shopping list](/changelog/v0.1.0/list.png)

### Know what you can cook

Every planned meal wears a cookability badge: green means you have everything, so the "what's for dinner" question answers itself. As you tick items off in the store, badges update live.

### Free, and great on your phone

Cuicuit is free to use, funded by supporters instead of subscriptions. Install it on your phone (it works as an app, no app store needed) and take it grocery shopping — search, filters, and shortcuts are all thumb-friendly. New accounts start with example recipes so the app is never empty on day one.

### Also new

- Full recipe filters plus search on mobile, and custom ingredients for anything missing from the catalog.
- Recipes without photos get a pretty ingredient grid instead of a blank box.
- Shopping list display settings, custom aisle order, and a clearer mobile header showing your active space.
- A public roadmap on the landing page so you can see what's coming — plus a demo video if you want to watch before signing up.
- For the tinkerers: a public REST API and an MCP endpoint so scripts and AI agents can drive Cuicuit too.
